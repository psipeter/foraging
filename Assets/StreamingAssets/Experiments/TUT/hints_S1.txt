# Tutorial hints. A block is a trigger line, then the popup: title, text, and a "Press [Enter] to ..." prompt. Each block is shown once.
#
#     @bout 1
#     Your first harvest
#     You earned {points} points.
#
#     Press [Enter] to continue
#
# Triggers (Assets/Scripts/UI/TutorialHints.cs; an unknown one is an error): bout n (a harvest that took fruit ends), patch_switch n (the nth qualifying patch leaving, as the selections count them),
# selection_start n, selection_end n, time_left s (the first moment s foraging seconds or fewer are left; the clock pulses yellow while the hint is up). A waiting foraging hint holds back a selection hint behind it; after a resume, counts carry on from the highest hint of a trigger already shown.
# {points} is what the harvest that fired it earned.

@bout 1
Harvesting

Harvesting this bush earned {points} points
You can **stop** harvesting a bush anytime, or **return** to a bush later

Try harvesting a different bush

Press [Enter] to continue

@bout 2
Features

Harvesting this bush earned {points} points
A bush's **shape** and/or **color** determine how many points you get

Try harvesting a few more bushes, then move to a new area

Press [Enter] to continue

@selection_start 1
Selection

**Select** the most valuable bushes based on what you learned

Press [Enter] to continue

@selection_end 1
Selection

You earn up to **100 points** for selecting better bushes
These **hidden** points are added at the end of the session

Press [Enter] to continue

@time_left 90
Scoring

You have a limited **time** in each session
Learn and harvest **efficiently** to earn more **points**

Press [Enter] to continue

@time_left 30
Pausing

You can **pause** the game at any time
The timer stops while you are paused

Press [Escape] to pause
