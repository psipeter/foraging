<size=28><b>Welcome to the Foraging Task</b></size>

Move the mouse to look around. Press W to walk forward and S to walk backward.
Bushes glow as you approach them. When a bush pulses, hold Space to harvest it.
You cannot walk while harvesting, and harvesting pauses if you look away from the bush.
Collect as much reward as possible before sunset.

<i>Press Space to begin.</i>
